1. What is the roller game?

What is ROLLER?

ROLLER is a 2D platformer where the player must get through levels and reach the checkpoint at the end.

2. What are you changing?

A one sentence summary describing what you’re changing.

I am adding extra levels to the game as well as a new platform type that has separate rules from the basic platforms.

3. Which aesthetic does your mod achieve?

One word from that list of 8.

Discovery

4. Mechanic one

Describe the first main change you’re making, and how it interacts with the player.

The first mechanic is a level screen where players can select levels to play through, with the levels being locked until the previous level is completed. This can make the player feel as if they need to go through the entire game and unlock all of the levels.

5. Mechanic two

Describe the second main change you’re making, and how it interacts with the player.

The second mechanic is a new type of platform that the players can walk on, however if they land onto the blocks after falling or jumping, the platform will disappear after 1 second.

6. How will a player interact with your mechanic?

the dynamics you predict, the ways players actually play your game.

Players may find these mechanics and want to figure out how they will. They may want to discover all of the levels and how to use the breakable platforms for shortcuts or strategies.

7. What states does your mechanic progress through?

your state map from yesterday

My first mechanic has two states, one where the player can see the menu and cannot move and one where they can’t see the menu and can move. These states are triggered between them by the e key. My second mechanic also has two states, one where the breakable platform is normal and can be moved on and the other where the breakable platform has disappeared and can't be used to walk on or jump off of.

8. How do your mechanics achieve your aesthetic?

How do your mechanics create dynamics that achieve the aesthetic you picked?

My mechanic achieves my aesthetic by directing the player to discover how the mechanics work. The level mechanic may drive the player to complete ROLLER and discover the layout of each of the levels. The breakable platforms mechanic may drive the player to discover what causes them to break and use them to discover secrets in the levels.
