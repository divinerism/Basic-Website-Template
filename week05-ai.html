At the moment, AI is taking in new information by using any sources it can get its hands on. If you have any works published online, any posts, notes, novels, art, it might’ve been taken as well. Many artists' works are used in order to train AI data sets without their explicit consent. For example, in an article on artists speaking out against the use of ai, David Baldacci, a bestselling novelist, spoke about how his entire body of work was used to train LLMs, large language models. This type of training was seen by him as a personal violation and professional threat to his works. He said that “‘In about five seconds three pages came up that had elements of pretty much every book I’d ever written...‘“

This theft of artists’ works to train AI is horrible for multiple reasons. Not only does it violate them and benefit unjustly from their hard work, it also takes away from their effort as the AI replicates their work in a fraction of the time. This can cause the real artist to be outclassed by the Generative AI stealing their work. Therefore,the artist may not be able to keep up with the AI, so their job may not be sustainable as consumers favor the AI version. Overall, this completely violates the artist and trivializes their work.

In my opinion, the public does not need access to Generative AI. While, yes, I am not going to say that places such as hospitals should not use AI in order to assist with procedures, or the use of AI in machines for precise movement, most citizens do not need this. In fact, a lot of the things that Generative AI is used for by citizens can be found through reading a few paragraphs of an article. I believe that the average civilian does not need to use Generative Ai and can instead look directly at the sources that those AIs pull from.
